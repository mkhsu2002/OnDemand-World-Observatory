## Eligibility risk that disclosure alone does not cure
The official consent attachment's originality clause includes **similar works by the author and works published online**, and excludes already issued products or commercial creations. The main rules require an unpublished, unentered, unexhibited and unawarded work. A different name, new repo, new proposal or prior-work label does not guarantee compliance. No explicit exemption for old K assets was found. Ask the organizer to confirm the exact new/old boundary and whether a proposal with prior concept footage is eligible. If no clearance arrives, do not sign a warranty you cannot truthfully make.

The attachment also mentions serious cases involving work done by others. Ask how AI-assisted application preparation and existing generation services are treated. This package transparently records AI-assisted drafting, diagram construction and interface/example preparation. M.K. Hsu must review and own the final submission claims.

## License exposure requiring owner decision
For winning entries, rules and attachment provide perpetual, royalty-free domestic/international commercial and noncommercial use, including modification and sublicensing. Submission itself permits event display and nonprofit promotion regardless of award. The consent form also licenses use of the participant's likeness, name and voice until written agreement ends it. Third-party permissions must support the organizer's intended scope. No term was accepted and no form was signed.
